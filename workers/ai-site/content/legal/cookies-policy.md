## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to website owners. worlddigital.ai uses cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage and pixel tags) to operate our platform and improve your experience.

## Types of cookies we use

### Strictly Necessary

These cookies are essential for the platform to function correctly. They enable core features like user authentication, session management, and security. You cannot opt out of these cookies.

- Session token
- CSRF protection token
- Cookie consent preference

### Performance & Analytics

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our platform by collecting aggregated, anonymous information. This helps us improve page performance and user experience.

- Page view counts
- Feature usage metrics
- Error tracking

### Functional

Functional cookies allow the platform to remember your preferences and personalize your experience, such as language settings, dashboard layout, and notification preferences.

- UI preferences
- Dashboard layout
- Notification settings

### Marketing & Targeting

These cookies may be used to serve you relevant advertising and track marketing campaign performance. They are placed by third-party advertising networks we partner with.

- Google Analytics
- Meta Pixel
- Snapchat Pixel

## Third-party cookies

Some cookies on our platform are set by third-party services we use, such as Google Analytics, the Meta Pixel, and Snapchat Pixel. These third parties have their own privacy policies governing their use of cookies. We recommend reviewing their policies for more information.

## How to manage cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or delete cookies that have already been set. Be aware that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our platform.

To learn more about managing cookies in your browser, visit the relevant help pages:

- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/kb/clear-cookies-and-site-data-firefox)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471/mac)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)
