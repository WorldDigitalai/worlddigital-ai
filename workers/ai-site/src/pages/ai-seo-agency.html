FAQ

Common questions.

What does an AI SEO agency do?

An AI SEO agency improves how search engines and AI assistants find, understand and recommend your business: fixing your website’s titles, content and technical issues, adding structured data, keeping your Google Business Profile and listings consistent, and checking how ChatGPT and Google’s AI answers describe you.

Do you offer generative engine optimization (GEO) services?

Yes. GEO services are part of the plan: we make your business facts consistent across the web, publish clear answer-style content and add structured data so ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews can find and cite you.

Do you work with businesses in Canada?

Yes. We provide AI SEO and local SEO services for businesses across the US and Canada, fully remote.

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO is the practice of making your brand visible inside AI-generated answers from tools like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini and Perplexity. It focuses on clear, well-structured, trustworthy content and consistent brand information across the web, so AI systems understand your business and cite it when people ask questions in your field.

What is AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)?

AEO means structuring your website so search engines and voice assistants can pull direct answers from it. That includes question-and-answer content, concise explanations, schema markup and fast, well-organized pages, which help you appear in featured snippets, “People also ask” boxes and voice search results.

How is AI SEO different from traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO aims to rank your pages in Google’s blue links. AI SEO also prepares your content to be understood, summarized and recommended by AI assistants and AI-powered search. We combine both: keyword research, technical SEO and content optimization, plus AEO and GEO so your business shows up wherever customers search.

They pull answers from pages they can crawl and trust: clear, specific content that answers the question, structured data, and business details that match across your website, Google Business Profile and directories. GEO work makes your business easy for them to find, understand and quote.

How do you measure AI search visibility?

Each month we run the same set of customer questions through ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews and record whether your business is mentioned or cited, next to Google rankings, clicks and leads from Search Console and GA4. You see the trend, not a one-off screenshot.

Can you guarantee my business will appear in ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews?

No honest AI SEO company can guarantee placement, because the AI tools decide what to show. What we can do is remove the reasons they skip you: missing or inconsistent business facts, thin pages, blocked crawlers and no reviews. That is what moves the numbers.
