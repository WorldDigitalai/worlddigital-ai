Short answer

How much does AI SEO cost?

Most small businesses will see four price levels. Gig sellers (Fiverr and similar) charge roughly $50 to $600 per order. Low-cost monthly plans run about $100 to $500 a month. Productized AEO/GEO packages run about $500 to $2,500 a month. Full-service agencies start around $1,500 to $5,000 a month for small businesses and go far higher for larger companies. How to choose

5 questions to ask any AI SEO provider.

  1. What exactly happens each month? Ask for a task list, not just “optimization”.
  2. How do you measure AI visibility? A good answer names the AI tools and the questions checked, and shows a before-and-after.
  3. Is there a long-term contract? Long lock-ins are a red flag for small budgets.
  4. Who owns the work if I leave? Your website changes, content and listings should stay yours.
  5. Do you build links, and how? Cheap link packages can hurt more than help.

Is $200 a month enough for AI SEO?

For most local and small businesses, yes, if the money goes to the basics that move results: a complete Google Business Profile, fixed technical and on-page issues, answer-ready FAQ content with schema, and consistent business facts across the web. What is the difference between AEO, GEO and AI SEO pricing?

They are usually sold together. AEO (answer engine optimization) targets direct answers and featured snippets, GEO (generative engine optimization) targets mentions in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, and AI SEO is the umbrella term. Our plan covers all three at one price.

Are Fiverr AI SEO gigs worth it?

They can work for a one-off task such as a quick audit. For ongoing visibility, the risks are inconsistent quality, no ownership of the strategy, and low-quality links that can hurt rankings.
