What is GEO?

Being the answer, not just a link.

Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the work of making a brand easy for AI systems to find, understand and trust, so they name it in generated answers. AI assistants build answers from what they can read on your website and what other sources say about you. If that information is thin, inconsistent or hard to parse, someone else gets recommended.
