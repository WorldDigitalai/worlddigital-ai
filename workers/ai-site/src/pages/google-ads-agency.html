FAQ

Common questions.

How much does Google Ads management cost?

It depends on your goals, monthly ad budget and campaign complexity. After a short call, we send a custom proposal with clear deliverables, so you only pay for what you need.

What do your PPC management services include?

Account audit, keyword research and negative keywords, ad copy and testing, landing page advice, conversion tracking in GA4, bid and budget management, Local Service Ads where eligible, and a clear monthly report on leads and cost per lead.

How do you measure results?

Every campaign is tracked with GA4 and performance dashboards. We monitor metrics such as CTR, CPA, ROAS and conversions, run A/B tests, and share clear performance reports with data-driven recommendations.

Can you take over my existing ad accounts?

Yes. We start by auditing your current campaigns, tracking and spend, then prioritize the fixes and opportunities with the biggest impact.

Do you offer Google Ads management outside Houston?

Yes. We manage Google Ads for businesses anywhere in the US and Canada: keyword research, ad copy, landing pages, conversion tracking in GA4 and a clear monthly report on leads and cost.

Can I run Google Ads alongside SEO?

Yes. Many clients run both: Google Ads brings calls and bookings right away while SEO builds rankings that keep paying off. Google Ads management is quoted separately after a short conversation.
