Results & reporting

How long before I see results?

Google Business Profile and on-page fixes can show results within weeks. Competitive rankings usually build over three to six months. Google Ads can bring calls in the first week.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings on Google?

No. WorldDigital.ai does not guarantee first-page Google rankings, and you should be careful with anyone who does. Google decides rankings, and AI assistants like ChatGPT decide what they cite, so no honest company can promise a position. What we commit to is the work: the fixes, content and listings that move rankings, and a monthly report showing exactly what changed.

How will I know what you did?

Every month you get a one-page report: what we changed, how your traffic, calls and rankings moved, and what we will do next.

Can I still make changes to my website or ads myself?

Yes. Your accounts are always yours. Just let us know so we can account for it in the plan.
