What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO is the practice of making your brand visible inside AI-generated answers from tools like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini and Perplexity. It focuses on clear, well-structured, trustworthy content and consistent brand information across the web, so AI systems understand your business and cite it when people ask questions in your field.

What is AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)?

AEO means structuring your website so search engines and voice assistants can pull direct answers from it. That includes question-and-answer content, concise explanations, schema markup and fast, well-organized pages, which help you appear in featured snippets, “People also ask” boxes and voice search results.
